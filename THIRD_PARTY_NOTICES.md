# Third-Party Notices & License Compliance Report (`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`)

This document provides a comprehensive license audit and attribution registry for **TypesetOK (TOK)**, covering:
1. The repository's own governing license (`TOK-NCCL v1.1`).
2. All third-party Rust crates declared in `Cargo.toml` and resolved in `Cargo.lock`.
3. All third-party Node.js / TypeScript packages declared in `package.json` and resolved in `package-lock.json`.
4. All bundled Hebrew and UI fonts in `assets/fonts/` and `assets/fonts/ui/`.
5. Legal compliance observations and flagged clauses requiring attention prior to commercial or public distribution.

- **License Category:** **Source-Available, Non-Commercial Copyleft** (Dual-Licensed for Commercial Use)
- **Full Text:** See [`LICENSE.md`](./LICENSE.md)

### Important Legal Classification & Flagged Clause Notes

1. **Source-Available vs. Open Source (OSD #6):**
   - `TOK-NCCL v1.1` is a **Source-Available** license, **not** an OSI-approved Open Source license or FSF Free Software license. Section 3 (*Strict Non-Commercial Restriction*) prohibits Commercial Use without a separate written commercial license agreement, which intentionally restricts field of endeavor (Open Source Definition #6).
   - Documentation, package metadata, and public announcements must refer to TypesetOK as **"Source-Available (Non-Commercial Copyleft)"** rather than "Open Source".

2. **Section 3.2 ("Commercial Output Clause") Enforceability & Scope:**
   - Section 3.2 of `LICENSE.md` states that *"Commercial Use"* includes using the Software to typeset, paginate, or generate PDF/X, HTML, or `.tok` files for commercially sold books, periodicals, or paid client work, or use by any entity with annual gross revenue exceeding `$10,000 USD`.
   - **Flagged Legal Consideration:** Under copyright law in several jurisdictions (including U.S. copyright jurisprudence regarding computer program output), the output generated by a software tool (such as a layout or PDF document containing the user's own text) is generally not a derivative work of the software engine itself unless the output embeds copyrighted creative expression belonging to the software licensor. Consequently:
     - Enforceability of output restrictions relies primarily on **contractual acceptance** (EULA / clickwrap / terms of use) rather than pure copyright infringement remedies in jurisdictions that distinguish tool code from user-authored output.
     - Furthermore, the bundled fonts in `assets/fonts/` are licensed under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`OFL-1.1`)**, which explicitly grants unrestricted permission to use, study, copy, and embed the fonts in any document (commercial or non-commercial), provided the fonts themselves are not sold by themselves. The `TOK-NCCL v1.1` output restriction applies solely to the use of the TypesetOK software engine under contract/license, and does **not** alter or restrict the independent `OFL-1.1` rights governing the third-party font files.

3. **Section 14 (Third-Party Compatibility):**
   - Because `TOK-NCCL v1.1` imposes non-commercial restrictions, it is **incompatible with strong copyleft licenses** such as `GPL-2.0`, `GPL-3.0`, `AGPL-3.0`, or `LGPL-3.0` (when statically linked) that forbid adding further restrictions beyond the GPL.
   - **Audit Result:** A full audit of `Cargo.lock` and `package-lock.json` confirms that **zero** GPL, AGPL, or LGPL libraries are linked or bundled in TypesetOK. All third-party dependencies use permissive licenses (`MIT`, `Apache-2.0`, `BSD-2-Clause`, `BSD-3-Clause`, `ISC`, `Zlib`, `Unicode-3.0`) or Public Domain (`SQLite`), which are fully compatible with distribution alongside `TOK-NCCL v1.1`.
